Administrative regions derive from geoBoundaries gbOpen, William & Mary geoLab and contributors (CC BY 4.0 compatible). Source-specific license, attribution, dates, immutable download links and checksums are retained in `data/source/gbOpen/index.json` and `manifest.json`. Geometry has been clipped to supplied country outlines; overlapping regions were rejected and gaps preserved as residual territories. These modifications are not authoritative political boundaries.

Attribution: Runfola et al. (2020), geoBoundaries: A global database of political administrative boundaries. PLOS ONE 15(4): e0231866. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0231866
