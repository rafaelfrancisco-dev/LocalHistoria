# World Bank Open Data

`data/source/country_stats.json` is a snapshot of the World Bank Open Data API, retrieved on the date recorded in the file:

- World Development Indicators: SP.POP.TOTL, NY.GDP.MKTP.CD, NY.GDP.MKTP.KD.ZG, SP.POP.GROW, PA.NUS.FCRF
- Worldwide Governance Indicators: GOV_WGI_PV.SC (Political Stability and Absence of Violence/Terrorism, 0–100 score)

The World Bank publishes this data under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/public-licenses). Source: The World Bank. LocalHistoria transforms it: it takes the latest available year for each indicator, fills gaps with estimates, splits national totals across regions, and evolves the values during a campaign. The World Bank has not endorsed these changes.
